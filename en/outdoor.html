Outdoor Military Equipment

Stop 10 of 10

Missiles, boats and armoured vehicles in the open air: military technology at full scale.

Large-scale equipment

Visit Astana identifies an outdoor pavilion as part of the museum, where twentieth-century weapons and military equipment are displayed.

The open-air display includes a surface-to-air missile launcher with four missiles pointing skyward, a Coast Guard boat numbered 607 resting on steel cradles, and rows of armoured vehicles lined up on the paved plaza, with the city’s buildings behind them.

What the outdoor area adds

Large-scale equipment is difficult to present inside a conventional gallery. An outdoor display allows visitors to understand the physical scale and design of military vehicles and other equipment.

Visitor route

After exploring the indoor halls, visitors can use the outdoor area as the final part of the museum route. Bronze horsemen beside the entrance steps make a fitting place to begin or end the visit.
