Atrium “Mangilik El”

Stop 1 of 10

Every visit begins here: under the dome, in front of a bronze monument that ties the country’s ancient warriors to its modern armed forces.

Historical continuity

The atrium is the central space of the museum. E-Museum describes it as a place where the history of military heritage is told through a sequence that connects ancient warriors with the modern period, including a series of reliefs that show how weapons evolved.

The centrepiece is the Мәңгілік Ел (“Mangilik El”, or “Eternal Country”) composition: a group of bronze soldiers gathered around a map, framed in carved stone with a cityscape behind them. Figures of warriors stand on either side, so the whole room reads as a conversation between eras.

Before you go further, look for the scale model of the complex, with its blue dome, its lawns and the tiny tanks and aircraft parked outside. It helps you picture the outdoor display before you reach it.

What to look for

  • Representations of warriors from different historical periods
  • Reliefs showing changes in weapons
  • The central “Mangilik El” composition
  • Connections between historical military traditions and modern Kazakhstan
