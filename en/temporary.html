Temporary Exhibitions

Stop 9 of 10

A hall that changes: special collections and new research, without disturbing the permanent chronological route.

Changing exhibitions

The museum includes a space for temporary exhibitions. E-Museum identifies this as a separate hall and gives the example of a temporary exhibition featuring a collection from the “Empire” antiques salon.

Purpose

Temporary exhibitions allow museums to introduce new themes, collections and research without changing the permanent chronological structure of the main halls. A visitor who has already walked the permanent route once can still find something new here.

Where it sits

The temporary-exhibitions hall is part of the same building complex that contains the permanent halls, a concert hall and the home of the Central Military Orchestra and the Central Ensemble of the Ministry of Defence.

For visitors

Because temporary exhibitions change, visitors should check current museum information before planning a visit. The Plan a Visit page lists the phone numbers published by e-Museum.
