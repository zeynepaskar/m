Visual Arts

Stop 8 of 10

Paintings, graphics and sculpture on the theme of defending the homeland — including dioramas that step out of the frame.

Art and military history

The visual arts hall contains paintings, graphics and sculptures connected with the theme of defending the homeland. E-Museum highlights portraits and sculptural representations associated with historical figures including Zhambyl Zhabayev, Tomiris, Manshuk Mametova and Aliya Moldagulova.

Paintings you can walk up to

Some of the most memorable works are dioramas: a painted panorama continues into a sculpted foreground of rubble, weapons and figures, so the picture seems to reach into the room. Elsewhere, framed portraits — such as a soldier in a greatcoat — give a single face to a period.

Forms of art

  • Painting
  • Graphics
  • Sculpture
  • Portraits
  • Historical compositions

Why art is included

Art gives visitors another way to encounter historical memory. It can show how military events, people and ideas have been represented in different periods.
