Weapons of the XX–XXI Centuries

Stop 6 of 10

From automatic rifles to aviation equipment: the hardware of the twentieth and twenty-first centuries.

Weapons collection

E-Museum describes this hall as a collection of twentieth- and twenty-first-century weapons and military technology. It includes pistols, automatic weapons, grenade launchers, anti-tank mines, aviation weapons and models of military equipment.

Categories

Small arms
Individual weapons used by military personnel.
Anti-tank weapons
Weapons and devices designed for use against armoured vehicles.
Aviation weapons
Equipment associated with military aviation.
Military equipment models
Models that help visitors understand the development of military technology.

Big pieces

Not everything here fits in a glass case. A field gun on spoked wheels stands on the marble floor, and an aircraft cockpit with an ejection seat lets you see the scale of the equipment that pilots used.

Historical context

The history of military technology is one of the components of military history identified in academic research on Kazakhstan.
